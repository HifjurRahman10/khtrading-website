About KH Trading

A Canadian company focused on exporting pulses to international markets.

KH Trading is a Canadian pulse exporter based in Toronto, ON, Canada. We connect international buyers with Canadian-origin lentils, peas, chickpeas and other pulses suited for export.

What we export

Our focus is on staple pulses commonly shipped from Canada, including red and green lentils, yellow and green peas, chickpeas and related pulse crops.

Markets we serve

We work with buyers who source Canadian pulses for food manufacturing, distribution and retail supply chains, with a focus on South Asia.

Our approach

We source Canadian-origin pulses through established supply channels and align product specifications and export documentation with buyer requirements. Logistics are coordinated with freight partners through to destination markets. Engagement follows a clear path: inquiry, product confirmation, commercial terms, then shipment coordination.
