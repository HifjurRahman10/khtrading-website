Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2025

This policy describes how KH Trading (“we”, “us”) handles personal information collected through www.khtrading.ca. We are a Canadian business operating in Ontario, Canada.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form, we may collect your name, email address and the content of your message. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on this site at this time.

How we use information

We use contact details only to read and respond to your inquiry and to maintain ordinary business records related to that communication. We do not sell personal information.

Form processing

Messages submitted through the contact form are sent by email to KH Trading for review and response.

Retention

We keep contact messages only as long as needed for business correspondence and reasonable record-keeping, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your rights

Depending on applicable Canadian privacy law (including provincial legislation where relevant), you may request access to or correction of personal information we hold about you. Contact us at hifjur@khtrading.ca.

Changes

We may update this page from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
